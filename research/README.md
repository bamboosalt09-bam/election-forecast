# Research records

This directory is explicitly outside the active packaged V27 runtime. It keeps
superseded figures and other historical research records that remain useful for
methodological transparency but must not be confused with current public
outputs.

Canonical frozen model artifacts remain under
`outputs/active_presidential_nested_v23` through `v27`. The corrected 2025
demonstration remains under `outputs/prospective_pres_2025_v27`. New ablations
must use a new versioned research output and may enter the active path only
through documented promotion, regression and frozen-boundary checks.
